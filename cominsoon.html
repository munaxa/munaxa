The trust layer

Reliability is a feature,
not a vibe.

Every customer carries a reliability score that moves with their behaviour. Every shop carries protections. Together they decide what a booking means.

Score grows with kept appointments.

+2 per completed visit, +1 for fast rebook, bonuses for verified ID and tenure. Trusted customers unlock auto-approval and deposit waivers.

Score drops on misses and late cancels.

−4 for inside-window cancels, −12 for unannounced no-shows. Repeat offenders trigger deposit holds or get blocked from booking at your shop entirely.

Shops set the rules.

Threshold for auto-approval, deposit floors, blocklist windows, who pays for what. Defaults that just work, overrides for shops with opinions.
